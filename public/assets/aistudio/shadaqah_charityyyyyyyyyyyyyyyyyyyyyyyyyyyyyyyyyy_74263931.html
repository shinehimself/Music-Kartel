Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum donation?

$10 or €10, or the local equivalent shown in the portal for your country.

How do I donate from outside Africa?

Use the UBA bank wire details in the portal, then send your transfer receipt to our WhatsApp so we can allocate your gift.

Can I choose where my money goes?

Yes. Pick any of the nine programs, or give to the general $10 / €10 fund.

Can I give monthly?

Select Monthly Sadaqah Jariyah and pledge via WhatsApp; our team will arrange a recurring option with you.

How do I request aid?

Fill in the aid request form. Our field committee reviews applications weekly and contacts you by mobile.

How can I volunteer or partner?

Use the Partner or Volunteer form in the Vision section, or message us directly on WhatsApp.
