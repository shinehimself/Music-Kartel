Part One

The Constitution of Sadaqah Africa Charity Organization

Governing Charter

Preamble

“In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. We, the founders, trustees, volunteers, and ground allies of Sadaqah Africa Charity Organization, affirming that every step in the right direction matters, adopt this Constitution to guide our collective duty of relieving distress, restoring human honor, and building a brighter, more equitable world for all.”

Article 1 Name, Headquarters & Legal Character

The organization is formally designated as Sadaqah Africa Charity Organization (herein referred to as “the Organization” or “SACO”). The principal headquarters and registered secretariat is situated in Jinja, Uganda, Africa, with legal mandate to establish field offices, coordination hubs, and volunteer alliances across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and the wider East and Sub-Saharan African region.

Article 2 Non-Profit Nature & Prohibition of Personal Gain

The Organization is established strictly as an independent, non-governmental, non-profit humanitarian institution. No part of its net earnings, collected donations, or accumulated capital shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, any founder, trustee, officer, or private individual, except as reasonable and audited reimbursement for legitimate operational field expenses directly incurred in advancing relief.

Article 3 Core Humanitarian Mission & Nine Essential Pillars

The Organization’s primary purpose is grassroots relief across 9 core pillars:

  • 1. Deep-water community boreholes & clean sanitation
  • 2. Pediatric sickle cell therapy, surgery & medicine
  • 3. Food parcels & emergency malnutrition relief
  • 4. Full annual orphan boarding, welfare & education
  • 5. Women’s dignity sanitary kits & maternal hygiene
  • 6. Rural Madrasa learning slates, books & facilities
  • 7. Village mosque construction & community centers
  • 8. Sunnah Qurbani / Eid halal livestock distribution
  • 9. Weatherproof shelter, durable mattresses & insulated blankets

Article 4 The Sacred Principle of Amanah & Designated Allocation

Every single donation received by the Organization constitutes an inviolable religious and ethical trust (Amanah). Funds earmarked by a donor for a specific purpose (such as a water well, sickle cell surgery, or schoolroom) shall be segregated and spent exclusively for that specified project without diversion or dilution.

Article 5 The $10 / €10 Campaign & Grand $50,000 Milestone Architecture

The Organization establishes the $10 Sadaqah Campaign to democratize charitable giving. Contributions of $10 are aggregated into 5 audited milestones scaling up to $50,000:

$10,000 13 Boreholes
$20,000 28 Sickle Cell Ops
$30,000 Food Security
$40,000 Orphan Care
$50,000 Empowerment

Article 6 Major Capital Endowments: Schools ($20k–$70k) & Hospitals ($43k–$150k)

The Organization is chartered to accept, construct, and steward permanent educational and healthcare complexes:

  • School Construction ($20,000 to $70,000): Ranging from starter 3-classroom primary blocks to complete 10-classroom secondary & vocational centers with science labs and clean water boreholes.
  • Hospital & Healthcare Facilities ($43,000 to $150,000): Ranging from maternal & pediatric community clinics to 50-bed multi-specialty general hospitals with twin operating theatres, oxygen generators, and 4x4 ambulance integration.
  • All major endowments include permanent carved stone dedication plaques and lifetime trust deeds vesting ownership in the local community.

Article 7 Field Committee & Beneficiary Verification Protocol

An autonomous Field Committee convenes weekly (every Tuesday) in Kampala to audit all aid requests logged online or via field workers. Priority is strictly allocated based on verifiable vulnerability: orphans, destitute widows, acute sickle cell emergency patients, and water-starved rural settlements.

Article 8 Board of Trustees & Executive Signatories

The Board of Trustees provides fiduciary oversight. No single individual can authorize disbursements independently. All financial expenditures and bank wire releases require two authorized signatories (the Executive Director / CEO and the Chief Financial Officer / Board Treasurer).

Article 9 Financial Administration & Official Banking Coordinates

All domestic and international donations are received into the designated NGO account held with United Bank for Africa (Uganda) Limited:

Account Name: Sadaqah African Charity Organization

Account Number: 0764007338 (UBA Kampala)

SWIFT / BIC: UNAFUGKA

Audit Standard: International Financial Reporting Standards for Non-Profits (IFRS)

Article 10 Anti-Corruption, Bribery Prohibition & Zero Leakage

The Organization enforces an absolute zero-tolerance policy against bribery, extortion, kickbacks, and administrative waste. Facilitation fees are strictly forbidden. Any trustee, staff member, contractor, or volunteer found violating this policy shall be immediately dismissed and reported to law enforcement.

Article 11 Mandatory Independent Annual Audits

The books of accounts of the Organization shall be audited annually by a reputable, independent firm of Certified Public Accountants licensed by the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Uganda (ICPAU). The audited financial statements must be made publicly accessible to all donors and stakeholders.

Article 12 Amendments & Asset Dissolution Safeguard

This Constitution may only be amended by a two-thirds majority vote of the Board of Trustees in consultation with founding patrons. In the event of dissolution, all remaining assets shall be transferred to a registered non-profit charitable institution pursuing similar Islamic and humanitarian relief objectives in East Africa.
